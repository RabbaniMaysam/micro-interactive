Chapter 1: Limits, alternatives, and choices

Individuals have endless desires but a limited budget. The tools in this chapter show what that limit looks like.

Budget line

You have a budget and can spend it on burgers or cans of soda

Every combination of burgers and soda that costs exactly your budget lies on the thick line. Combinations below the line are affordable (shaded); those above it cost more than you have. Move the sliders to change the budget and the two prices.
